Technical notes

Built to be placed.
Tested to be useful.

Verified engineUnity 6000.6.7f1 · Universal Render Pipeline 17.6.0
Verified runtimeWindows x64 · Mono · Direct3D11 · Both demo scenes tested
Unity content40 prefabs, 10 flat-color materials, Overview and CafeUseScene scenes
Textures & UVsNo image textures required. UV0 mapping with automated overlap, bounds and degeneracy checks. Generated lightmap UVs.
Scale & collisionMeter-based dimensions. 40 static MeshColliders passed Editor raycast checks.
Not includedRigging, animation, LODs, gameplay, liquid simulation and interactive equipment. Menu-board marks are decorative geometry.
Other platformsBuilt-in, HDRP, Custom RP, Unreal Engine, mobile and other desktop targets remain untested.
The collection uses original procedural geometry generated in Blender. OpenAI Codex assisted design, Blender scripting, export automation, QA and documentation. Preview images show the actual supplied models. No competitor geometry or paid third-party models were incorporated. Performance measurements are specific to the test hardware and are not gameplay FPS guarantees.
