# Growth execution plan — Hearth & Sage game assets

> Status: first Cozy Café collection is presented as preparing for Unity Asset Store review. Do not imply approved marketplace listings, downloadable packages or sales unless independently verified.

## Offer: ship usable game props, not pretty renders
**Audience:** indie Unity/Unreal developers building cozy café, shop or life-sim scenes.

**Core outcome:** "Populate a consistent cozy café scene with original low-poly props and clear compatibility documentation." Claims about engine compatibility must be grounded in real import tests; platform/editor version and render pipeline must be specified.

**Offer ladder:**
1. Free: public real-engine screenshots and a **verified** small sample package when available.
2. Core paid pack: Cozy Café props, clear SKU, number of actual unique meshes, verified formats, tested render pipeline, license and changelog.
3. Expansion: matching restaurant, bakery or mini-shop props, only when initial buyers show demand.
4. Studio bundle: consistent style packs and updates after repeat sales are observed.
Pricing must be compared against live marketplace offers; do not guess revenue.

## Leads: show the work inside a real engine
- Create a truthful 30–45 second engine walkthrough from installed real assets.
- Publish a spec table: engine versions, formats, poly-count ranges, scale, UV/material approach and included items.
- Pick specific high-intent long-tail terms (e.g., "cozy cafe low poly prop pack Unity URP"), research competition before making claims.
- Community posts should teach building a small café scene, with a clearly disclosed relevant link when allowed.
- Offer one useful sample scene or free prop subset if the original asset license allows it.

## Metrics
Site `collection_view` → `sample_download` (real file only) → `marketplace_click` → verified marketplace transaction.
Measure actual product-page impressions, downloads, sales, refunds, feedback, ratings (where platform supplies them). Never fake showcase screenshots or testimonials.

## 14-day validation
Week 1: compare 10 competing asset packs and 3 common indie dev scenarios; choose a differentiated micro-scene.
Week 2: finish and verify a real mini-scene, share a tutorial in permitted channels, record qualified clicks and feedback.

**Go/no-go:** Proceed to mass production only after multiple qualified developers test the sample and at least one provides a concrete purchase signal. Otherwise adjust formats/style/use case first.

## Integrity requirements
No fabricated marketplace approval, fake product reviews or unsupported "works everywhere" compatibility statement. Do not substitute AI concept illustrations for actual 3D assets.
